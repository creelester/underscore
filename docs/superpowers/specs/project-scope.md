# Underscore - Project Scope

## Problem

Stories are more involving when told with emotions and mood.
We see this happening in movies: through acting, the characters deliver emotions, and through, music the director delivers the mood that the viewer is supposed to experience.
What if this happened with books too? Adding a soundtrack that matches the mood of the book we are reading could add a new layer of involvement for the reader.

## Solution

Underscore is a mobile app that creates beautiful cinematic, mood setting playlists to go with your reads.
You look up the book you're reading and through Underscore's AI-powered playlist-building system, you get the perfect playlist for your book to play in the background while you read.
The playlist will match the genre, find an OST if exists already, use references to similar playlists to improve the content you receive and allows you to share the playlist and play it on your preferred music streaming app.

## Features

- Search for books manually (by title and author) or by scanning the book cover and fetch metadata
- Analyze the overall genre, the mood by chapter and the feel and rhythm (calm vs upbeat vs stressful vs happy)
- Create a playlist specific for the book
- You can share the playlist with other users
- You can open it in Spotify, Apple music, Youtube music, Amazon Music (kindle + amazon music !)
