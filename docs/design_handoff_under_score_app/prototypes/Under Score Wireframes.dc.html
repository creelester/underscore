1Under Score — user flow & wireframes

WHAT I ASSUMED

  • Audience: casual readers who want ambience with zero effort — so the happy path is search a title → one tap → playlist in Spotify. Mood tuning exists but is optional and comes with a proposed answer already filled in, never a blank form.
  • Entry: title/author search only (Google Books). No cover scan this pass.
  • Playlist: fixed ~30 Claude-curated tracks resolved against Spotify search, per your working conventions. No ratings, no runtime sizing.
  • Nav: 3 tabs — Now (current book + player), Library, Profile. Generation is a full-screen flow launched from Now/Library, not a tab.
  • Fidelity: grayscale, 390×844 iOS metrics, 44px+ targets, real brand-voice copy. Blue text is my annotation, not UI. Colour, mood gradients and the DS components arrive in the hi-fi pass.

OPEN DECISIONS FOR YOU

  • Mood capture — three models: 1e chips, 1f sliders, 1g AI-proposes. For a zero-effort audience I'd ship 1g with 1e as its "advanced" escape hatch.
  • Re-scoring as the story shifts — three models in 1h. This is where the product could get complicated fast; I'd start with model 1.
